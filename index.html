Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying, selling, or renting

What documents are required to purchase a property?

Usually, buyers need a valid ID, proof of address, bank statements, and, depending on the transaction, a purchase offer and proof of funds.

Do you offer property inspections?

Yes. We arrange guided inspections, condition reviews, and neighborhood checks so clients can make informed decisions.

Do you help clients living abroad?

Absolutely. We support remote buyers with virtual tours, secure document handling, and dedicated advisory throughout the process.

How long does the buying process usually take?

It varies by property and documentation, but many transactions are completed in 2 to 8 weeks with the right preparation.

Do you manage rental properties?

Yes. We provide tenant placement, rent collection, property maintenance coordination, and full property management support.
